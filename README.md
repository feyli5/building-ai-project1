# Smart Shelf Life - Building AI Project
### 1. Your Idea in a nutshell* **Project Name:** Smart Shelf Life* **Description:** A simple computer vision app that tracks the freshness of grocery items in a home kitchen to reduce daily food waste.
### 2. Background* **Problem:** Household food waste is incredibly common and expensive because people forget about items hidden in the back of the fridge. * **Motivation:** Automating this tracking helps families save money and reduces carbon footprints from discarded waste.
### 3. Data and AI techniques* **Data Sources:** Image datasets of everyday fruits, vegetables, and packaged food items.* **AI Techniques:** Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for image recognition to identify the type of item, and object detection to classify its freshness status based on surface changes.
### 4. How it is used* **Context:** Users scan items using their mobile phone camera before placing them in the pantry or refrigerator. * **Affected Users:** Busy individuals and families who manage household cooking.
### 5. Challenges* **Limitations:** The AI cannot accurately judge structural spoilage underneath the packaging or deep inside unpeeled food items. It relies entirely on visual surface signs.
### 6. What next* **Future Growth:** Integrating the application directly with local smart refrigerators or connecting it to digital store receipts to automatically populate items upon checkout.
### 7. Acknowledgments* **Credits:** Built using open-source pre-trained computer vision libraries like OpenCV and TensorFlow.
